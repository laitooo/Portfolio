Privacy Policy — Naggo

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Overview

Naggo is designed to work primarily on your device. We do not require an account, and we never sell your personal information or the content you create. The priorities and settings you enter stay on your device. Anonymous crash reports and usage statistics are collected as described in section 5.

2. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in the app — priorities, notes, completion history, quiet hours, cadence and other settings — is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded by us anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing its storage) permanently removes this data.

3. Permissions

The app asks for permissions only when a feature needs them, and every permission is optional:

  • Notifications — used only to deliver the hourly nudge you asked for.
  • Exact alarms (Android) — used only so the nudge fires at the cadence you set instead of drifting.
  • Ignore battery optimisations (Android) — optional; requesting this makes the nudge more reliable on aggressive power-saving devices.
  • Boot completed (Android) — allows the app to reschedule your nudge after the phone restarts. It runs no code otherwise.

Naggo can appear in your system share sheet. When you share text into Naggo from another app, that text is passed to the “Add priority” screen so you can save it as a task. It is not sent anywhere else.

4. Notifications

Nudges are generated on your device by the local notification system. They contain the titles of your top open priorities, drawn from the list you keep in the app. They are never routed through our servers — there is no push service and no cloud in this path.

5. Diagnostics

To fix bugs and understand which features matter, the app uses two Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — if the app crashes, a report is sent containing technical details such as the error, stack trace, device model and operating system version. Your priorities and notes are not included.
  • Firebase Analytics — the app logs anonymous feature events such as “a task was added”, “a task was finished”, “the nudge was toggled” or “a notification was opened”. These events record that something happened, not what you wrote — no titles, notes or other content of your priorities are ever sent. Analytics also collects standard technical data (device model, OS version, language, approximate region from your IP, and a random installation ID) that is used in aggregate.

This data is processed by Google (Firebase) on our behalf under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The app contains no advertising and does not share data with advertisers. To request deletion of diagnostics data associated with your device, email us using the address in section 8 and reference the approximate dates.

6. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and is suitable for general audiences. The diagnostics described in section 5 are anonymous and apply equally to all users.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page and included in an app update, together with a new “last updated” date above.
